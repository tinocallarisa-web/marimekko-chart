## Description

```
A stacked chart tells you who leads each segment. It does not tell you which segments matter. Supermarkets and corner shops get the same column width, so a strong position in a small channel looks as good as a strong position in the channel that carries half the market.

Marimekko & Mekko Chart by TCViz draws both at once: the width of each column is the size of the segment, the stack is who shares it, so the area of every cell is its share of the whole market. Power BI has no built-in Marimekko chart; this one answers "where are we strong in a big market?" in one picture.

READ THE MARKET

• Column width = size of each segment, with its total and its % of the market above it
• Cell labels with the share of the column, hidden when they do not fit
• Others is never dropped: the series beyond the named ones are added up, so every column keeps its real 100%
• The tooltip gives both readings: share of the column and % of the total
• Order columns by size or data order; order series by size or data order

PRO

• 4 to 12 named series (the free version names 3 plus Others)
• Your company highlighted, the others dimmed, and a summary sentence: its share, its strongest and its weakest segment
• Change in share against last year or any comparison period, in points on every cell, with the segment that explains it
• Columns ordered by your share; small multiples on a shared scale
• Horizontal bars and absolute values (bar Mekko); labels with the value or the % of the total

FREE AND PRO

The free version is a complete, correct Marimekko chart: columns at 100% with three named series and Others, share labels, totals, axis and legend. Pro adds the reading of your own position: focus, change and comparison. Turn on a Pro capability while editing without a licence and it is drawn working, on your own data, under a "Pro preview" watermark that names it; reading view shows the free result.

WORKS LIKE A NATIVE VISUAL

• Click a cell, a column label or a legend item to filter the page; Ctrl+click for several; Others filters by every series inside it
• Highlight from other visuals, bookmarks restore the selection, drilldown on a hierarchy, Power BI context menu
• Keyboard navigation, screen-reader descriptions and high contrast
• The number format of the measure, dynamic format strings included; Format pane and texts in 12 languages
• Six style templates, including an IBCS style; format on the canvas; a setup guide and a start screen

PRIVACY

The visual makes no network requests and declares no privileges, so Power BI does not let it send anything anywhere. Everything is computed inside your report.

GETTING STARTED

1. Columns: the segments of the market — channel, region, category.
2. Series: who shares them — company, brand, competitor.
3. Value: sales, volume or units.

Documentation, how to read a Marimekko chart and FAQ: https://tinocallarisa-web.github.io/marimekko-chart/support.html
Questions: https://github.com/tinocallarisa-web/marimekko-chart/discussions
Support: support@tcviz.com
```
