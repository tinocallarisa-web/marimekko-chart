WHAT TO CHECK
1. Empty visual: start screen; resize it (three layouts).
2. Sample report: Columns = Subchannel, Series = Manufacturer, Value = Market sales (M€).
It opens with Focus series = Iberia Drinks (Pro): in edit mode the watermark shows;
clear it for the free result, 3 named series plus Others.
3. More Pro: Small multiples = Year; Layout > Horizontal; Focus series = Begudes Nord.
4. Click a cell: other visuals filter by its column and series (every identity; Others
carries all its series). A column label or legend item selects the whole column or
series. Click again to clear; Ctrl+click adds. Bookmarks restore it.
5. Keyboard: arrows move between cells, Enter selects, Esc clears. High contrast.
6. Format mode: click a cell, a column label, the axis, the legend or the background.
7. New in 1.0.1: Format > Accessibility (high contrast, focus, minimum text, patterns);
Cell color fx (rule per cell); axis titles; dynamic format strings per cell.
