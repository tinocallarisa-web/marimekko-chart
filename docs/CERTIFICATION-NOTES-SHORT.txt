WHAT TO CHECK
1. Empty visual: start screen; resize it (three layouts).
2. Sample report: Columns = Channel, Series = Manufacturer, Value = Market sales. Column
width = channel size; 3 named series plus Others in the free version.
3. Pro, editing without licence: Format > Focus series = Begudes Nord; Comparison value =
Market sales PY; Small multiples = Year; Layout > Horizontal. Watermark appears.
4. Click a cell: other visuals filter by that column and series (every identity of the cell;
Others carries all its series). Click a column label or a legend item to select the whole
column or series. Click again to clear; Ctrl+click adds. Bookmarks restore it.
5. Keyboard: arrows move between cells, Enter selects, Esc clears. High contrast.
6. Format mode: click a cell, a column label, the axis, the legend or the background.
