YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION — FULL VIDEO
==========================================

A stacked chart tells you who leads each segment. It does not tell you which segments matter. Marimekko & Mekko Chart by TCViz shows both at once: the width of each column is the size of the segment, the stack is who shares it. Power BI has no built-in Marimekko chart.

──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT DOES
──────────────────────────────────────────
✅ Column width = size of each segment, with its total and its % of the market
✅ Cell labels as share of the column, % of the total, value, or series name
✅ Columns or bars; 100% shares or absolute values (bar Mekko)
✅ Others is never dropped: every column keeps its real 100%
✅ Six style templates, including an IBCS style, and a setup guide inside the visual
✅ Click to filter, highlight, bookmarks, drilldown, keyboard navigation and high contrast
✅ The measure's number format, dynamic format strings included; twelve languages
✅ No network requests: everything is computed inside your report

──────────────────────────────────────────
FREE AND PRO
──────────────────────────────────────────
FREE — a complete Marimekko chart:
• Columns at 100%, three named series, the rest in Others
• Share labels, column totals with their % of the total, share axis, legend, templates

PRO — read your own position:
• 4 to 12 named series
• Your company highlighted, with a summary sentence: share, strongest and weakest segment
• Change in share against a comparison period, in points per cell
• Small multiples on a shared scale
• Horizontal bars, absolute values (bar Mekko), value and % of the total labels

Turn on a Pro feature while editing without a licence and it is drawn working on your own data, under a "Pro preview" watermark.
